Sustainability Projects

As part of our sustainability and community service activities, the Global Impact Youth Club organized a school cleaning event. During this activity, club members worked together to clean different areas of the school compound.

The goal of this event was not only to keep our school environment clean but also to support our school cleaners. Students personally helped the cleaners with their daily tasks so that their workload would become easier.

During the activity, the cleaners were able to sit down and take a rest while students helped with the cleaning work. This was our way of showing appreciation and respect for the important work they do every day for our school community.
