Special Events

Date: October 10, 2025

Teacher’s Day was celebrated in the staff area after class. There were food items including popcorn, traditional Ethiopian bread, coffee, and other treats. In addition to the food, the celebration featured a ketema (a type of grass) and an etan, a fragrant Ethiopian smoke, creating a warm and cultural atmosphere honoring our teachers.
