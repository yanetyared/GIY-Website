Technology Projects

GIY offers a comprehensive Technology course that includes coding, AI, and digital innovation. Below are the course modules:

Lesson Description
Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning The goal of this lesson is to help students understand the basics of artificial intelligence and how machines think. Real life examples like chat bots, voice assistants, and translation apps. Simple ideas of algorithms and data. Introduction to machine learning with the help of YouTube recommendations. AI in daily life: smart homes, phones, and social media. Impacts of AI in our day to day life.
Automation The goal of this lesson is to show how machines and robots make work more efficient and effective. What is automation? What is the difference between manual and automated work? Automation in factories: robots assembling cars, drones, packages, phones, etc. Automation in transport: self driving cars, drones, and delivery systems. Impacts of automation on human lives.
Future Tech and Innovation The goal of this lesson is to explore new technologies shaping our world and how to stay updated. Space Tech: satellites, rockets, and how these innovations help us. Green Tech: solar panels, wind energy, electricity. Bio Tech: new medicines, food science, lab grown meat. How to spot new trends concerning technological advancements.
Hands on tech skills The goal of this lesson is to set the right path for students who wish to enter the field of technology. Getting started with web development and web design (HTML & CSS). Basics of app development and design. Coding logic and programming languages (Python, Java, C, C++).
Class Work Students will write a short essay on the topic they were most interested in.

Note: These lessons are given to students by students and reviewed by teachers in the field of technology.

Students who participate in the GIY Technology Course receive certificates recognizing their learning and participation at open house. These certificates are given to students who actively attend the lessons and complete the course activities.

The certificate acknowledges that the student has participated in learning topics such as Artificial Intelligence, Automation, Future Technologies, and basic technology skills like web development and coding logic.

As part of the learning experience, GIY organized an educational trip collaborating with IT club for students to the Science and Technology Museum in Addis Ababa.

During the visit, students explored different exhibitions related to science, technology, and innovation. The trip helped students connect the knowledge they learned during the course with real-world scientific and technological developments.
