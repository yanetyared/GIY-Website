What is GIY?

The Global Impact Youth Club (GIY) is a youth-led initiative founded in Ethiopia with the mission of empowering young people to become active drivers of positive change. GIY is more than just a club — it is a movement where passionate and creative youth come together to learn, lead, and act on issues that matter most to their communities and to the world. We believe that young people are not just the leaders of tomorrow, but also changemakers today, and we provide them with the skills, opportunities, and platforms they need to thrive.

Benefits You Will Get After Being a Member

  • Skill Development: Gain practical skills in technology, leadership, communication, project management, and problem-solving.
  • Networking & Mentorship: Connect with like-minded youth, leaders, and professionals.
  • Volunteering Opportunities: Participate in community service projects to create real change.
  • Exclusive Opportunities: Access competitions, conferences, and international youth collaborations.
  • Recognition & Certificates: Receive awards and certificates that strengthen your profile.
  • Supportive Community: Become part of an inspiring family of changemakers.

Long Term Goals

  • Build Community Tech Hubs: Establish innovation centers with resources and mentorship.
  • Promote Sustainability: Launch environmental projects like tree planting and recycling campaigns.
  • Expand Leadership Programs: Train youth through leadership academies.
  • Strengthen Volunteer Networks: Grow networks of volunteers across Ethiopia and beyond.
  • Expand International Collaborations: Partner with youth clubs and NGOs worldwide.
